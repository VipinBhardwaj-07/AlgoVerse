Privacy Policy

AlgoVerse is a learning platform for data structures and algorithms. This policy explains what information is used and why.

Information we handle

  • Account & profile details (name, email, password, profile information you add) are stored in your browser's local storage.
  • Contact messages you send through the contact form are delivered by EmailJS.
  • AI assistant messages you send to Vera are processed by our server and the Groq API to generate a reply.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. AlgoVerse does not store your card details.
  • Usage analytics are collected through Google Tag Manager to understand how the site is used.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, export your data, or delete your account from Settings → Data & legal. Privacy controls are in Settings → Privacy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: officialalgoverse@gmail.com

Terms of Service

  • Don't misuse the platform, attempt to disrupt it, or access accounts that aren't yours.
  • Paid plans are billed through Stripe. Refund and cancellation terms should be stated here before launch.
  • AlgoVerse is provided “as is”. We work to keep it accurate and available but can't guarantee uninterrupted service.

Cookie Policy

AlgoVerse uses browser storage to keep you signed in and remember your settings. Google Tag Manager may set cookies for analytics. You can clear site data in your browser at any time, and manage analytics in Settings → Privacy.
