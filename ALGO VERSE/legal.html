Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Policy information. This page describes the browser storage and analytics currently documented for AlgoVerse. Have it reviewed by a qualified professional before relying on it as a legally complete policy.

Browser storage and cookies

AlgoVerse stores account and preference information in your browser's local storage to support sign-in and remember settings. Local storage is not itself a browser cookie, but it is data stored on your device and can be cleared through your browser's site-data controls.

Google Tag Manager and analytics tags configured through it may use cookies or similar technologies to understand site usage. The specific cookies depend on the tags enabled on the site.

You can clear cookies and other site data in your browser settings. Doing so may sign you out or reset preferences. Privacy controls are available in Settings → Privacy.
